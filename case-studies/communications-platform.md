# AI-Assisted Communications Platform

## Overview

Built and supported a calling and contact-management platform for a United States-based company. The platform combined telephony, an AI voice agent, agent workflows, dashboards, metrics, and business-system integrations.

This case study is a sanitized representation of professional work. Client names, source code, credentials, recordings, prompts, customer data, and proprietary architecture are intentionally omitted.

## Context

The operation needed a way to collect initial caller information, route calls to the appropriate teams, manage active calls, and track activity across the support workflow. The platform served a Level 1 operation, a Level 2 escalation team, and Level 3 escalation through Salesforce.

## Challenge

- Collect initial caller information before agent handoff.
- Support a high-volume calling workflow of approximately 200 calls per day.
- Provide agents with a softphone and dashboard for call administration.
- Support escalation, queue overflow, callbacks, and outbound calls.
- Make operational activity visible through tracking and metrics.
- Integrate the workflow with Salesforce.

## Solution

Implemented and supported a Node.js calling system using Twilio for IVR and an AI voice agent as the first point of contact. The platform connected the initial call flow with agent-facing tools, dashboards, metrics, and business-system integrations.

The solution supported callback, barge-in, and outbound calling capabilities. It also provided routing and escalation support across the operational levels, including Salesforce-based Level 3 escalation.

## Scale and outcome

- Supported approximately **200 calls per day**.
- Supported a Level 1 operation with **12 agents**, with approximately **4–5 connected simultaneously**.
- Supported a Level 2 escalation team with **10 agents**, with approximately **2–3 connected simultaneously** for escalations and queue overflow.
- Enabled tracking, callback, barge-in, and outbound calling features that were previously unavailable or not functioning.
- Connected the workflow to Salesforce for Level 3 escalation.
